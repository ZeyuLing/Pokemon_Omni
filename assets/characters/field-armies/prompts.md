## Soldiers — final source

This reference is the scale and shape authority. Do not draw long legs, tall torso, realistic proportion or detailed concept art. The final characters should be ONLY 20 LOGICAL PIXELS TALL and 14-16 pixels wide, large 10-pixel-wide head occupying almost HALF the height, torso only 5 pixels, legs only 5 pixels. Enlarge logical pixels uniformly without smoothing. Output transparent background. Four rows, four columns, EXACTLY 16 isolated sprites. Equal cells, ample clear gutters. Each row a different military uniform, columns DOWN-facing front, UP-facing back, LEFT-facing profile, RIGHT-facing profile. Row1 Kanto charcoal and red military infantry helmet, steel shoulders, red scarf, pokeball belt. Row2 Hoenn dark teal uniform and steel marine helmet, tan backpack harness. Row3 Sinnoh blue-grey mountain uniform, fur collar and gold epaulettes, helmet. Row4 Unova dark navy and purple uniform, peaked cap, brass belt, short purple cape. No weapons, no logos or text. Each soldier is a Pokemon trainer in military uniform. The defining requirement is to MATCH THE REFERENCE'S CHIBI ORIGINAL GAME MAP SPRITE silhouette: wide head and very short body, not a thin tall illustrated soldier. Flat 15-color palette per regional uniform, bold big clean hard pixel clusters. Every sprite same 20 pixel visible height, same bottom baseline, fixed cell spacing. Use reference image for proportions and pixel art style, not its clothing.
